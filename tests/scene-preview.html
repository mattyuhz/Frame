Test the tricky parts.

Eight compact videos for trying scene suggestions and a scrubber that focuses on one section. Every clip is original synthetic footage with known edits, no audio, and no personal content.

  1. Preview a clip below, then choose Download video.
  2. Open Frame and choose the downloaded file. On iPhone, save it to Files first.
  3. Turn Sections on. When multiple sections are available, choose one to scrub in detail. Whole video restores the full timeline.
  4. Choose Find scenes when you want automatic suggestions. Opening a file or turning Sections on does not start a scan.
  5. To inspect a boundary, select the second or a later section and choose Review start. Compare before/after frames, adjust with −10 / −1 / +1 / +10, then choose Apply boundary or Cancel.

Frame reads the chosen file on your device; it does not upload your video. Every new file starts with Sections off. Playback stops at the selected section's end. Editing pauses a scan; Continue scan resumes it. Turning Sections off stops scanning and restores the full timeline, while retaining prepared sections for this file. Scene suggestions are approximate, so short cuts and fades can be missed.

Applying a boundary keeps both sections connected and leaves the source video unchanged. The scan button becomes Manual boundaries: further scanning is disabled to protect your edits. Turning Sections off/on keeps them; reopening the file resets them. Frame steps remain approximate for variable-rate footage, and before/after review does not guarantee a correct cut.
